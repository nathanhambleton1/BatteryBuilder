UNDERTOW / FIELD MANUAL

Know the depths.

01 / CONTROL YOUR BUOYANCYHold Space, W, Up arrow, the rise button, or touch the water to ascend. Release to descend. Down arrow or S dives faster. Heavier boats answer the helm more slowly. In open water you can rise all the way to the surface and ride it with the snorkel open to refill air, but mine cables reach the surface too.
02 / THREAD THE MINEFIELDSPass between paired mines. A mine strike costs two hull sections, a tether costs one. Rock is fatal, including the island cliffs above each cavern mouth. On higher levels some mines drift with the current. Captain and Admiral also face hunter submarines: a glowing curved path warns where their fast torpedo will travel. Move clear of the path or shoot it down.
03 / WATCH YOUR AIROxygen drains the whole dive. Steer through glowing air pockets to refill it. If it runs out, the hull implodes.
04 / HUNTERS ABOVEOnce you are deep into the patrol, warships appear on the surface and drop homing missiles. Their seekers start crude and grow sharper the farther you go. Ramming a ship from the surface costs two hull sections. Every hit costs a hull section and knocks out a system: sonar goes dark, a breach leaks air, or the ballast jams. Lose every section and the boat is gone.
05 / RETURN FIREF, X or right-click launches a missile. Captain and Admiral carry three separate shots: press R or tap RELOAD to refill the tubes, including a partial magazine. It locks onto an incoming missile first, then a warship, then the nearest mine ahead. Reload time depends on your boat, so pick your moment.
06 / SPECIAL ABILITYEvery class carries one unique ability: a torpedo salvo, flank speed, silent running, repairs and more. Press E or tap the ability button to trigger it, then wait for it to recharge. When the light fades, sonar pings on its own and reveals mines with returning echoes.
07 / LEVELS AND SCORINGCadet, Officer, Captain and Admiral raise speed, enemy fire and air drain. Score is distance plus mines cleared, ships sunk and missiles intercepted, multiplied by level (0.75x to 1.8x). P / Esc pauses, M toggles sound, N toggles music.
08 / CAMPAIGNOperation Deep Meridian is 24 missions in six chapters, the same course for every sailor. Pass the light curtains to save a checkpoint; a lost boat restarts at the last one. Q (or the DRONE button) sends the salvage drone to a wreck in range, or launches the spotter drone when you ride the surface at a recon point. Beacons only count once the spotter is back: press Q to turn it around and land it on the deck before the battery runs out. Hunter subs, destroyers and worse wait between checkpoints, and every mission runs at Admiral pace. Spend salvage in the Workshop. Boats unlock by player level across all modes, with the regular fleet available by level 45 and Turtle at level 75. Switch boats in the Dry dock, and earn up to three stars per mission: finish it, finish it in one run, and find Dr. Vance’s hidden journal page. Progress is saved under your callsign.
09 / ORBIT CAMERAThe camera swings around your boat in 3D. Hold A / D or the left and right arrows to orbit; it eases back when you let go. C switches to the classic flat side view.
10 / 3D SALVAGE DRONEQ at a salvage site releases the ROV through a short deployment sequence. WASD flies forward, backward and sideways. Space rises, C descends, and Shift boosts. Drag the view or use the arrow keys to look around. Q scans; hold F near a payload to work the tool. Release thrust to stabilize. Follow the green recovery marker and hover slowly inside the dock with your cargo. Touch controls provide movement, ascent, descent, sonar and the tool. Recall safely abandons the sortie; the site stays available for another attempt.
DRY DOCK / SELECT VESSEL

Choose your boat.

Each hull is modelled on a real submarine class. Bigger boats carry more armour, air and launch tubes but need a wider gap and turn slowly.
