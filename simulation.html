UNDERTOW / FIELD MANUAL

Know the depths.

01 / CONTROL YOUR BUOYANCYHold Space, W, Up arrow, the rise button, or touch the water to ascend. Release to descend. Down arrow or S dives faster. Heavier boats answer the helm more slowly. In open water you can rise all the way to the surface and ride it with the snorkel open to refill air, but mine cables reach the surface too.
02 / THREAD THE MINEFIELDSPass between paired mines. A mine strike costs two hull sections, a tether costs one. Rock is fatal, including the island cliffs above each cavern mouth. On higher levels some mines drift with the current.
03 / WATCH YOUR AIROxygen drains the whole dive. Steer through glowing air pockets to refill it. If it runs out, the hull implodes.
04 / HUNTERS ABOVEOnce you are deep into the patrol, warships appear on the surface and drop homing missiles. Their seekers start crude and grow sharper the farther you go. Ramming a ship from the surface costs two hull sections. Every hit costs a hull section and knocks out a system: sonar goes dark, a breach leaks air, or the ballast jams. Lose every section and the boat is gone.
05 / RETURN FIREF, X or right-click launches a missile. It locks onto an incoming missile first, then a warship, then the nearest mine ahead. Reload time depends on your boat, so pick your moment.
06 / SPECIAL ABILITYEvery class carries one unique ability: a torpedo salvo, flank speed, silent running, repairs and more. Press E or tap the ability button to trigger it, then wait for it to recharge. When the light fades, sonar pings on its own and reveals mines with returning echoes.
07 / LEVELS AND SCORINGCadet, Officer, Captain and Admiral raise speed, enemy fire and air drain. Score is distance plus mines cleared, ships sunk and missiles intercepted, multiplied by level (0.75x to 1.8x). P / Esc pauses, M toggles sound, N toggles music.
08 / ORBIT CAMERAThe camera swings around your boat in 3D. Hold A / D or the left and right arrows to orbit; it eases back when you let go. C switches to the classic flat side view.
DRY DOCK / SELECT VESSEL

Choose your boat.

Each hull is modelled on a real submarine class. Bigger boats carry more armour, air and launch tubes but need a wider gap and turn slowly.
