NEREID / FIELD MANUAL

Know the depths.

01 / CONTROL YOUR BUOYANCYHold Space, W, ↑, the rise button, or the water to ascend. Release to descend. ↓ / S dives faster. Smooth, short holds give the best control.
02 / FIND THE OPENINGPass between paired mines. Mines, tether cables and rock are solid. Openings follow a connected route; speed rises and gaps narrow as you travel.
03 / LISTEN AFTER DARKSunset begins around 240 m. Automatic sonar comes online as the light fades. A wave strikes a mine, then its returning echo briefly reveals it. Press E or tap sonar for an extra pulse when ready.
04 / INTO THE UNKNOWNThe seabed rises and falls. Caverns and deep basins alternate with open water. P / Esc pauses, M toggles sound. Practice makes collisions harmless and does not save a record.
